# New starter IT checklist

## Before day one

IT prepares a laptop for every new starter at Larkspur Labs. Your manager files an Onboarding ticket at least five working days before your start date, with your name, team and the software you need. Laptops are imaged on Thursdays, so late requests may wait for the following week.

## Day one

Collect your laptop from the IT desk on floor 1 between 9:00 and 10:00. Sign in with the temporary password on the card, then choose a new password straight away. You will also be asked to enrol the authenticator app on your phone; without it you cannot use the VPN.

## First week

Install the tools your team uses from the Software Portal. Anything not listed there needs approval: licences under 200 pounds a year are approved by your manager, larger ones by the IT lead. Report problems with a Hardware, Network or Access ticket, and mention that you are a new starter so the ticket is handled first.

## Leaving the company

On your last day IT disables your accounts at 17:00. Return your laptop and any loaner devices to the IT desk before then so they can be securely wiped.
