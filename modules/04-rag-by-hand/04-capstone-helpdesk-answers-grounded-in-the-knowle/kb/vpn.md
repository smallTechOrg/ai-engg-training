# VPN access

Updated: 2025-04-02

Larkspur Labs staff must use the Larkspur VPN client to reach internal tools such as the wiki and the ticket system when working outside an office.

Install the client from the Software Portal and sign in with your normal username and the authenticator app code. Connections drop after 12 hours and you must sign in again.

If the VPN will not connect, check that your laptop clock is correct, then restart the client. Still stuck? File a Network ticket.
