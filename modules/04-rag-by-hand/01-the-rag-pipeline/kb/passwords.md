# Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must be changed every 180 days. You cannot reuse your last 10 passwords.

To reset a forgotten password, open the self-service portal at the address on the intranet home page and choose Forgot password. You will receive a code in the authenticator app.

After 5 wrong attempts your account locks for 30 minutes. IT can unlock it sooner if you contact the helpdesk and confirm your identity.
