# Printers

There is one shared printer on every floor. Add it from the Printers page on the intranet; do not install drivers from the internet.

If a printer shows as offline, switch it off and on, wait two minutes, then remove and re-add it. Toner is replaced by Facilities, not IT: message Facilities in the helpdesk channel.

Colour printing is available only on floor 2 and is limited to 50 pages per person per month.
