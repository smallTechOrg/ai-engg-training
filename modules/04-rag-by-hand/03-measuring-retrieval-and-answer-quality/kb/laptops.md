# Laptop replacement

Larkspur Labs replaces laptops every 4 years. Your manager will be reminded 60 days before your laptop reaches its replacement date.

If your laptop is broken sooner, file a Hardware ticket. IT repairs it first; if it cannot be repaired, a loaner is issued the same day and a replacement is ordered.

Return the old laptop to IT within 14 days of receiving the new one so it can be securely wiped.
