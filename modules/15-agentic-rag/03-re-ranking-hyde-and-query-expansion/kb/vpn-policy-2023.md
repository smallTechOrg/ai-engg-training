title: VPN policy (2023)
effective: 2023-03-01
status: superseded by vpn-2025

Larkspur Labs staff must use the VPN whenever they reach internal systems from outside an office.

VPN sessions last 12 hours. After 12 hours the client signs you out and you sign in again with your password and a code from the authenticator app.

The supported client is the Heron VPN client, version 4. Install it from the software portal.
