id: vpn-2025
title: VPN policy (2025)
effective: 2025-09-01
status: current, replaces vpn-2023

Larkspur Labs staff must use the VPN whenever they reach internal systems from outside an office.

VPN sessions last 8 hours. After 8 hours the client signs you out and you sign in again with your security key. Authenticator codes are no longer accepted.

The supported client is Larkspur Connect. The old Heron VPN client was retired on 2025-10-31 and no longer connects.
