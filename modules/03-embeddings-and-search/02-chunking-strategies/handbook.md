# Larkspur Labs IT Handbook

## Passwords and accounts

Your Larkspur password expires every 90 days. To reset it, open the self-service portal and choose Forgot password. You will receive a code in the authenticator app. If your account is locked after five wrong attempts, wait 15 minutes or ask the helpdesk to unlock it.

Never share your password with a colleague, not even a manager. If you suspect someone else knows it, change it at once and tell IT security.

Two-factor codes come from the authenticator app. If you get a new phone, re-enrol the app before wiping the old one, because the codes are tied to the device. Without the old phone, the helpdesk must verify your identity in person before issuing a new enrolment.

## Network and VPN

The office Wi-Fi network is called Larkspur-Staff. Sign in with your normal username and password. Guests join the Larkspur-Visitor network; the daily code is printed at the front desk.

To work from home, install the Larkspur VPN client from the software portal and connect before opening internal tools such as the wiki, the ticket system or the file shares. If the VPN connects but internal sites do not load, disconnect, wait ten seconds and reconnect. Persistent failures should be reported with the error code shown by the client.

Slow Wi-Fi in a meeting room is usually caused by too many devices. Move to a wired desk or ask the helpdesk to check the access point on that floor.

## Laptops and devices

Laptops are replaced every four years. Ask your manager to request a new one through the hardware catalogue. A flickering or black screen should be reported as a hardware ticket; do not open the case yourself.

A lost or stolen phone or laptop must be reported to IT immediately so that it can be wiped remotely. Do not wait until the next working day. IT will lock the device, revoke its certificates and issue a replacement once your manager approves.

Printers: if a printer shows offline, restart it, then remove and re-add it from the Printers page. Paper and toner are kept in the supply cupboard on each floor.

## Software and access requests

New software is requested by filing a ticket with the business reason and the cost centre. Approved software is installed from the software portal within two working days. Open source tools still need approval because of licence terms.

Access to shared folders and mailboxes is granted by the owner of the resource. Ask the helpdesk for the owner's name, then ask the owner to approve the request in the ticket. Access is reviewed every six months and unused access is removed.

## Security basics

Report phishing emails with the Report button in your mail client; do not forward them. If you clicked a link in a suspicious email, tell the helpdesk straight away and change your password. Keep your laptop's disk encryption and automatic updates switched on. Restart after updates when asked; delaying more than three days locks the laptop until it is updated.
